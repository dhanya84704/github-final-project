Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, identity, or personal characteristics.

Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and ideas.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, discrimination, and sharing others' private information without permission.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may take appropriate corrective action when unacceptable behavior occurs.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related activities.

Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take the following actions depending on the severity of the violation:

1. Warning the individual.
2. Temporarily restricting participation.
3. Removing inappropriate content.
4. Permanently banning the individual from project participation.
